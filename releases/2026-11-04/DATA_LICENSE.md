Original software is released under the accompanying MIT licence. Original
analysis tables, annotations, figure source data and manuscript materials are
shared under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Third-party names, identifiers, metadata and attributed excerpts retain the
rights and access conditions of their original providers and publishers.
No licence to the full DrugBank, HMDB, Blood Exposome Database, NPAtlas or
publisher collections is granted by this archive. Download source releases
from the providers under their current terms. Licensed full texts and retrieval
corpora are not included.

- DrugBank: https://go.drugbank.com/releases/latest#open-data
