explanation: Quercetin 3'-O-glucuronide is a small molecule (Ref 1). It is a major metabolite of quercetin, formed in the human small intestine and liver by phase II biotransformation (Ref 2, Ref 3, Ref 4). It has been shown to have anti-inflammatory, anti-cancer, and antidepressant efficacies, and exerts cytoprotective effects in renal tubular cells (Ref 1, Ref 2, Ref 5). While quercetin itself is found in plants, quercetin 3'-O-glucuronide is a human metabolite, not solely exogenous (Ref 2, Ref 3). It is also not solely a microbiota-only metabolite, as it is formed by human phase II metabolism (Ref 2, Ref 3).
