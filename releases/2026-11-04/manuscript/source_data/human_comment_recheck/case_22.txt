It is produced in the human body through metabolism of sulindac by both gut microbiota and human enzymes (CYP450, FMO3). It functions in human biochemical and physiological processes by inhibiting COX activity, reducing prostaglandin synthesis, and exhibiting anti-inflammatory and anti-cancer effects.
